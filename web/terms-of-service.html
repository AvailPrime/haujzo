Haujzo Shipper Master Terms of Service

Version 2.0 • Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Binding commercial agreement governing individual shippers, corporate cargo owners, and authorized dispatchers booking commercial road freight transportation on Haujzo (com.haujzo.app).

1. Technology Marketplace Role (Not a Motor Carrier)

Haujzo ("Platform") provides digital freight-matching, Google Maps route estimation, live GPS telematics, waybill generation, and payment settlement software connecting cargo Shippers (on Haujzo) with independent commercial drivers and fleet carriers (operating on Haujzo Pro).

Haujzo is strictly a technology intermediary—NOT a motor carrier of record, common carrier, freight forwarder, customs broker, bailee, warehouseman, or direct insurer. Physical carriage is performed solely by the matched independent carrier.

2. Dual-Gate Eligibility & Didit KYC / KYB Verification

To book trucks on Haujzo, you must be at least 18 years old and possess legal capacity to contract. Shippers must complete BOTH: (a) automated identity/corporate and biometric liveness verification via Didit Protocol, AND (b) operational approval by Haujzo Portal.

3. Binding Freight Manifests, Weight & Container Declarations

You represent and warrant that every manifest detail—including cargo category, ISO container size/status (20ft, 40ft, Twin 20ft), container serial number, gross weight, itemized inventory, pickup/intermediate stop/dropoff coordinates, and consignor/consignee contacts—is true, lawful, and complete.

4. Dynamic Pricing, Multi-Currency Quotes & Paystack Settlement

  • Transparent Pre-Dispatch Quotes: Fares are computed dynamically from vehicle class, corridor distance tier, single or round-trip leg mode, ISO container multipliers or empty-return discounts, excess weight, road allowances, and optional fragile, hazardous, or loading crew selections.
  • Loaded-on-Truck Online Payment Rule: You may choose "Cash on Delivery (POD)" or "Paystack (Online)" at booking. Once the driver marks cargo as Loaded / Picked Up / In Transit, any unpaid booking triggers an online payment prompt via Paystack.
  • Anti-Double-Payment Rule: Once a trip is marked "PAID ONLINE", drivers are strictly forbidden from demanding physical cash.

5. Cancellation Cutoff, Refunds & Demurrage Windows

  • Self-Cancellation Cutoff: You may cancel in-app prior to cargo being loaded onto the truck. Once cargo is Loaded or In Transit, self-cancellation is locked and requires Haujzo Portal Admin review.
  • 120-Minute Loading & Offloading Windows: Every booking includes a free 2-hour (120-minute) loading window at origin and 2-hour offloading window at destination, audited via 500-meter GPS geofence timestamps. Excess delays incur hourly demurrage/detention charges.
  • Paystack Refunds: Eligible cancelled online-paid bookings are refunded to the original Paystack payment source or a verified saved Mobile Money / Bank wallet (matching Account Holder Name required).
