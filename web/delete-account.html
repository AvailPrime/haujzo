Haujzo Account & Data Deletion

Official account and personal data deletion instructions for the Haujzo — Truck Booking & Freight Logistics mobile and web application (com.haujzo.app).

Option 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your Haujzo account and associated personal profile data directly inside the mobile or web app at any time:

  1. Sign in to the Haujzo app.
  2. Go to Settings > Account Privacy & Security > Delete Account & Purge Data (or Profile > Delete Account).
  3. Confirm deletion and complete security re-authentication (Google Sign-In or Email & Password).
  4. Your account credentials, profile details, saved addresses, and refund wallet profiles are immediately deleted.

Option 2: Request Account Deletion via Web / Email (Without Opening the App)

If you no longer have the Haujzo app installed, you can submit a formal account deletion request from your registered email address:

Send an email to privacy@haujzo.com or support@haujzo.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request - Haujzo and include your registered email address or phone number.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained for Statutory Compliance

  • Permanently Deleted (within 30 days): Firebase Authentication credentials, full name, username, profile photo, email address, phone number, saved pickup/delivery addresses, and saved refund wallet profiles.
  • Retained / Anonymized for Legal & Tax Compliance: Completed commercial freight manifests, downloadable Waybill records, Paystack payment/refund transaction logs, tax audit records, and anti-fraud/chargeback dispute identifiers are retained or anonymized solely as required by statutory commercial transport, financial, and tax regulations.
