## 5. Google Play Console — App Content & Policy Declarations

### A. Permissions Hygiene (Why This App Passes Review Cleanly)
* **`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` & `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` (Kept):** Required for Google Maps pickup/intermediate/dropoff pinning, reverse geocoding, and live shipment map tracking while the user is actively using the app.
* **`ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` (Removed):** Removed from `AndroidManifest.xml` because the Shipper app only accesses device GPS in the foreground. Removing this avoids Google Play's strict Background Location Declaration rejection.
* **`CAMERA` & `RECORD_AUDIO` (Kept):** Used only when the user captures cargo/waybill documents, profile photos, or completes Didit WebRTC identity verification.

### B. Data Safety Questionnaire Mapping
When filling out **App content > Data safety** in Play Console:
1. **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** -> **Yes**
2. **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** -> **Yes** (HTTPS / TLS 1.3)
3. **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?** -> **Yes**
   * **Delete Account URL:** `https://haujzo.com/delete-account.html`

| Data Category | Data Type | Collected / Shared | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Approximate location, Precise location | Collected & Shared (with assigned driver for pickup/dropoff) | App functionality (Google Maps pickup/dropoff pinning & routing) |
| **Personal info** | Name, Email address, User IDs, Address, Phone number, Other info (KYC/KYB ID details) | Collected & Shared (consignor/consignee details shared with assigned carrier) | App functionality, Account management, Fraud prevention & security |
| **Financial info** | User payment info (saved refund MoMo/Bank account name/number), Purchase history | Collected (Processed via Paystack) | App functionality (Freight payment & refunds) |
| **Photos and videos** | Photos | Collected | App functionality, Fraud prevention (Profile photo, cargo photos, KYC ID scan) |
| **Files and docs** | Files and docs | Collected | App functionality (Waybills, cargo invoices, KYB documents) |
| **App info and performance** | Crash logs, Diagnostics | Collected | Analytics, App functionality (Firebase Crashlytics) |
| **Device or other IDs** | Device or other IDs (FCM push token) | Collected | App functionality (Push notifications for booking updates) |

### C. Target Audience & Content Rating
* **Target Age Group:** `18 and over` (Commercial B2B/B2C freight contracting requires 18+ legal capacity).
* **Ads Declaration:** `No, my app does not contain ads.`
* **App Access (Reviewer Instructions):**
  Provide Google Play reviewers with a pre-verified test Shipper account (Email + Password) in **App content > App access** so they can test signing in, viewing the Google Map, and filling out a freight booking form without needing to scan a physical government ID.
